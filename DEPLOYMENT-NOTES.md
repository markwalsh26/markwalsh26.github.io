## Service hours tally — accuracy rules
- Data is in service-metrics.json (aggregate only, no client names).
- Source: Compass CRM weekly invoicing workbook, 21 October 2026 version.
- Invoice Register: 253.75 included billable hours; subtract 3.0 hours for one short-notice cancellation and 4.0 hours recorded as training: 246.75 delivered client service hours.
- Site rounds down to nearest 10 and displays '240+' with 'recorded through 20 October 2026'.
- Browser fetches this JSON on each visit. It **does not** invent hours or increase over time. For weekly updates, reconcile completed shifts in the private CRM, edit this JSON's deliveredHours and asOf, and commit. Fully automatic updates require a private CRM-to-GitHub integration approved separately.
- Do not commit the underlying confidential CRM to this public repository.
