Our mission

Helping people move towards what matters.

Compass Support Services helps people stay active, connected and confident while continuing to make their own choices. Support may be arranged through the NDIS, aged care funding or directly as a private client.

Our approach is professional without feeling clinical or complicated. We start by listening, then shape practical support around the person’s routine, interests, goals and support requirements—not the other way around.

Our team comes from all walks of life and brings a breadth of backgrounds and experience. What unites us is a shared commitment to our values and a genuine passion for going above and beyond.

Our promiseFriendly, dependable support delivered with care, energy and respect.

Transport & outings

The right plan for the journey.

Getting there is part of the support. We can help plan transport for appointments, community activities, scenic drives, social events and everyday errands.

Options can include a comfortable hatchback for everyday trips, with other suitable arrangements considered for events, longer drives, additional passengers or equipment.

Everyday trips

Friendly, unrushed support for local appointments, shopping, activities and drives.

Events and longer outings

We plan the timing, comfort, breaks, passenger needs and practical details together.

Access comes first

We confirm mobility, access, storage and vehicle requirements before a booking so the arrangement is suitable.

Vehicle options are subject to availability and individual access needs. Please talk with us about the right arrangement for you.

Meet Mark

Mark Walsh Managing Director
