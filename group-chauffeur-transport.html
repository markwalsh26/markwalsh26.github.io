Important accessibility information

Let's confirm the right vehicle

Our current six-passenger van is not a wheelchair lift vehicle and is not suitable for remaining seated in a wheelchair during travel.

Our transfer equipment is a manual sliding transfer board, not an electric lift, powered platform or vehicle ramp. Suitable passengers move sideways across the board from their wheelchair into a fixed vehicle seat. Transfers are considered only after confirming the passenger's needs, seating suitability, safe handling plan, trained assistance and appropriate equipment.

Our current Mercedes-Benz six-passenger van does not have a wheelchair ramp or lift, and passengers cannot remain in their wheelchair while travelling. If a purpose-built wheelchair-accessible vehicle is required, please tell us when enquiring; that vehicle type is not currently advertised as part of our fleet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a group of six travel together?

Our van can accommodate up to six passengers, subject to seat availability, luggage, mobility needs and the agreed travel arrangement. Please confirm group numbers when enquiring.

Can someone who uses a wheelchair book the van?

Possibly, if they can safely transfer to and travel in a fixed passenger seat. Manual sliding transfer-board assistance is considered case by case; it enables sideways transfer to a fixed seat, not entry on a ramp or travel while seated in a wheelchair.

Can we book the vehicle for a private event?

Ask us about pre-booked chauffeur-style or group travel for events, social occasions and other journeys. Availability, vehicle/driver suitability, appropriate transport authorisations and a separate quote are confirmed before any booking is accepted.

Can NDIS or aged-care funding cover the transport?

It depends on the person's plan, purpose of the trip, transport arrangements and approved support. We explain agreed costs beforehand; luxury upgrades and ordinary private travel should not be assumed to be funded.

Tell us about your trip

Let us know your group size, pickup and destination, preferred time and any mobility or transfer needs. We'll discuss an appropriate option and confirm availability and pricing.
