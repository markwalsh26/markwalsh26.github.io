Personal Car Washing Support

Practical help to wash and maintain a personal vehicle for NDIS participants, aged-care clients and people booking privately—not a commercial fleet or detailing service.

Support with an important everyday task

For some people, washing a car involves barriers with mobility, reach, stamina, sensory needs, planning or safety. We can assist with the task or work alongside you at a pace that suits you.

Exterior washBasic wash, rinse, dry, windows and wheels by agreement.
Interior tidyVacuuming, wiping accessible surfaces and removing ordinary rubbish.
Do it togetherPrompts, sequencing and practical assistance to build participation.
Clear scopeTasks, products, water access and costs agreed in advance.

This is basic support for a participant’s personal vehicle. Paint correction, mechanical work, hazardous cleaning, commercial detailing and business fleets are excluded.

A careful note about NDIS funding

Car washing is not automatically NDIS-funded. A support must be a permitted NDIS support, directly related to disability needs, aligned with the participant’s plan and meet the funding criteria. Ordinary vehicle running or maintenance costs may remain a personal expense. Before booking, we can clearly describe the assistance requested so you can seek confirmation from your plan manager, support coordinator or my NDIS contact.

Check the official “Would we fund it?” guidance

Compass cannot determine eligibility or guarantee reimbursement. Written confirmation may be appropriate when there is uncertainty.
