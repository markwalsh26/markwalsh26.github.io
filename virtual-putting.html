Golf without the pressure

Virtual putting can make golf more approachable. Sessions can be short or extended, social or goal-focused, and adjusted for different experience, mobility and energy levels. No golf experience is needed.

Social connectionShare a relaxed activity and celebrate progress together.

How we can support the session

We can help organise a suitable venue, provide transport where agreed, support communication and equipment set-up, encourage breaks, and adapt the session around your preferences. Venue fees and availability are confirmed before booking.

Could NDIS funding help?

The NDIS may fund the extra disability-related support needed to participate in a recreational activity when it meets the funding criteria and aligns with your plan. Participants generally pay ordinary activity, entry and standard equipment costs themselves. We can help describe the support being provided clearly before you decide.
