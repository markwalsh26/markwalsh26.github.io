Moving & Relocation Support

Practical, one-to-one help to plan a move, organise the details and make a new home feel manageable.

Support before, during and after moving day

Moving can be especially demanding when mobility, energy, confidence or organisation creates extra barriers. We work alongside you and keep your choices at the centre.

PlanningTimelines, checklists, measurements, address changes and priorities.
Sort and packWork through belongings in manageable stages and label essentials clearly.
BookingsHelp research and contact suitable removalists, cleaners or other services with your approval.
Settle inUnpack everyday items, establish routines and organise the new space.

Clear service boundaries

Compass provides personal planning and practical support, not a removal truck or licensed removalist service. Heavy lifting, transport of household goods and specialist installations must be completed by appropriate providers.

Funding depends on individual circumstances. Confirm approval before booking funded support; private bookings are also welcome.
