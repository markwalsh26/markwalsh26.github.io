Home, Shopping & Daily Living

Flexible help to plan, learn and complete the routines that keep everyday life moving.

Do more of everyday life your way

Support can mean doing a task together, learning a new routine, using prompts or receiving hands-on assistance. We work with your preferences and focus on choice, safety and skills—not taking over.

Plan and shopLists, budgets, supermarkets, markets and online orders.
Meal routinesPlanning, simple preparation, kitchen organisation and clean-up.
Household routinesLaundry, changing linen, tidying and putting things away.
Home organisationDecluttering in manageable steps, recycling and weekly routines.

For a more thorough cleaning service, see our dedicated NDIS home cleaning support.

Using NDIS funding

Core supports may include assistance with daily life when it relates to disability support needs and the participant’s plan. Your budget may be flexible, but funding can only be used for permitted NDIS supports. We help explain the work clearly so you can check it before booking.
