Pre-booked Mercedes van travel for up to six passengers, including airport transfers, appointments and events. Vehicle, driver, insurance and booking requirements are checked before confirmation.

Mobility & transfer-board assistance

Where assessed as appropriate, a manual sliding transfer board can help a passenger move sideways from a wheelchair into a fixed van seat. The six-passenger Mercedes-Benz van has no powered lift or wheelchair ramp and cannot carry someone seated in a wheelchair. Individual assessment, safe transfer plans and trained assistance are required.
