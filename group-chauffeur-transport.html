Mobility support

Manual Transfer-Board Assistance

For suitable passengers, a manual sliding transfer board may assist a sideways transfer from a wheelchair into a fixed vehicle seat, following an individual safety assessment and agreed assistance.

Important: The van has no powered lift or wheelchair ramp and cannot carry someone seated in their wheelchair.

Passenger-transport bookings, vehicle suitability, driver credentials, insurance, availability and pricing are confirmed before travel. Funded support depends on each person's arrangements.
