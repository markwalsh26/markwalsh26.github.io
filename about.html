Our company

Good support should feel natural.

Compass Support Services helps people stay active, connected and confident while continuing to make their own choices.

Our approach is professional without feeling clinical or complicated. We listen, build genuine relationships and shape support around the person—not the other way around.

Our team comes from all walks of life and brings a breadth of backgrounds and experience. What unites us is a shared commitment to our values and a genuine passion for going above and beyond.

Our promiseFriendly, dependable support delivered with care, energy and respect.

Meet Mark

Mark Walsh Managing Director

I believe people thrive when they feel heard.

I bring 110% to what I do. My background in corporate leadership, coaching and mentoring taught me the value of listening, following through and helping people recognise what they are capable of.

Fitness and an active lifestyle are a big part of who I am. I bring that same energy and encouragement to supporting people as they build confidence, gain independence and try new things.
