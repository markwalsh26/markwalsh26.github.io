Home Cleaning Support

Reliable help with essential household cleaning for NDIS participants, aged-care clients and people booking privately—not a commercial office or large-property cleaning service.

A cleaner, safer and more manageable home

We start by understanding which household tasks you need assistance with and how you prefer them done. Support can be hands-on, alongside you or focused on building a repeatable routine.

Essential cleaningBathrooms, kitchens, surfaces, vacuuming and mopping.
Home routinesChanging linen, laundry, tidying and household reset tasks.
Your prioritiesA clear task list agreed before work starts.
Respectful serviceCare around belongings, privacy, preferences and your home.

We discuss products, allergies, access, pets and any safety considerations before the first service. Deep restoration, hazardous waste, pest work and specialist trades are outside this service.

Can NDIS funding cover cleaning?

Assistance with daily life may include a support worker helping with cleaning when the need relates to disability and the support is permitted by the participant’s plan. Ordinary living costs and services unrelated to disability are not automatically funded. We specialise in defining the disability-related household tasks clearly and can coordinate with your plan manager or support coordinator.
