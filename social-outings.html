Social Outings

One-to-one support for relaxed, meaningful time out in the community—based on what you enjoy.

Your interests lead the way

A social outing can be familiar and simple or a chance to try something new. We can help plan the details, get there, navigate the setting and make the experience feel comfortable.

Everyday connectionCafés, lunch, a scenic drive or visiting a favourite place.
Events and cultureCinema, live sport, exhibitions, music and community events.
OutdoorsParks, gardens, accessible walks and waterside outings.
Shared interestsGames, hobby groups, virtual putting or local clubs.

Support costs versus activity costs

If social and recreation support meets the NDIS funding criteria and aligns with your plan, funding may cover the extra support you need because of disability. The everyday costs everyone pays—such as admission, meals and membership—are usually paid by you.
