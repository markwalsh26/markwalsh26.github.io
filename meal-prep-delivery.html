Meal Prep & Delivery

Practical meal support built around your tastes, routine and level of independence.

Meals, your way

We can help with as much or as little of the process as you need. We agree the plan first, then keep it practical and flexible.

PlanMeal ideas, preferences, portions and a simple shopping list.
ShopSupport with groceries, online orders or collecting ingredients.
PrepareCooking, portioning, packaging and kitchen clean-up by arrangement.
DeliverPrepared meals can be delivered by arrangement where practical.

NDIS funding

Meal preparation assistance may be fundable where it is disability-related and consistent with your plan and the NDIS rules. Groceries and ingredients are personal expenses and are paid separately.

Every plan is different. We confirm the service, costs and what is being claimed before support begins.
