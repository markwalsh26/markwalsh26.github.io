Personalised assistance to make day trips, short breaks and travel experiences more manageable and enjoyable.

Turn an idea into a workable plan

Travel can involve many small decisions. We can help research options, build a realistic itinerary, prepare what is needed and provide agreed support during the experience.

Accessible planningRoutes, venues, breaks, facilities and backup options.
PreparationChecklists, packing prompts, bookings and travel-day routines.
Day tripsCoast, country, attractions, events or visiting family and friends.
Supported experiencesPractical assistance, navigation and confidence in unfamiliar places.

What funding may cover

NDIS funding may cover reasonable disability-related support required during an activity or trip when it meets the criteria and is included in the participant’s plan. Ordinary holiday expenses—including accommodation, food, tickets and standard travel costs—are generally personal costs. We itemise the proposed support so it can be checked in advance.

Read the NDIS guide to using funding

All support, rostering, safety arrangements and costs must be agreed before travel. Funding is not guaranteed.
