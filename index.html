A Team With More Options

Compass is a small, growing team of male and female support workers with a mix of strengths, backgrounds and interests.

The right fit

One-to-one assistance, active outings, household help, meal preparation and support with everyday tasks.

Transport choices

Cars, comfortable premium options and access to a six-passenger van for suitable group trips and events, subject to availability.

Mobility & transfers

Personal assistance and safe manual-handling practices within each worker's training and agreed support plan. Access requirements are checked before a booking.

For wheelchair-accessible or ramp-assisted travel, we confirm the specific vehicle, equipment, passenger needs and safe arrangements before accepting a booking.

Quick questions

Can I book Compass without an NDIS plan?

Yes. We offer private support as well as supports arranged through NDIS or aged care programs.

Can support include outings, work, study or practical admin?

Yes. We can discuss a tailored plan including activities, transport, everyday organisation and capacity-building tasks where appropriate.

Do you offer groups, vans or mobility assistance?

We can discuss suitable group transport and personal assistance. Availability, vehicle accessibility and safe transfer arrangements must be confirmed for each booking.
