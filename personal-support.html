Personal & Daily Living Support

Practical help with daily routines, shopping, meal preparation and other personal tasks, delivered with dignity, choice and clear safety arrangements.

Support shaped around your routine

Our team starts by listening. Together, we work out what assistance would be useful, how much independence you want to keep and how the support should fit your day.

Meal preparationHelp to plan groceries, prepare simple meals and build comfortable everyday routines.
Safe physical assistanceAgreed personal assistance within the worker's training, assessed handling requirements and a safe support plan.
Shopping and errandsPlanning shopping trips, collecting essentials and organising household items.
Household routinesPractical help with daily living, organisation and suitable home cleaning arrangements.

Any personal care, lifting or transfers must be within agreed scope and performed by suitably trained workers. Specialist clinical care is not represented as a general support-worker service.

Not sure what funding might cover?
