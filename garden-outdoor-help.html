Basic help—not specialist landscaping

Compass does not provide tree work, working at heights, chemical spraying, major landscaping, construction or licensed trade services. We discuss equipment, site access, weather and safety before confirming a job.

Funded eligibility varies by plan or aged-care arrangement. Private bookings are also available.
