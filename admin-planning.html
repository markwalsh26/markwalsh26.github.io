Practical assistance to organise everyday information, routines and appointments with less stress.

From overwhelming to manageable

We can break tasks into smaller steps, create a system that makes sense to you and work alongside you to keep it going. You make the decisions and approve any communication.

SchedulesCalendars, reminders, weekly routines and appointment preparation.
InformationSort ordinary paperwork, create checklists and find key documents.

This is everyday capacity and participation support. Compass does not provide legal, financial, accounting, medical or formal support-coordination advice.
